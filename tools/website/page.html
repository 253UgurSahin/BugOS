The story

Our rule: the Bugie test

“If Bugie doesn't get it, we make it again.”

That's the Bugie test. Every button, every word and every screen in BugOS has to pass it. If Bugie gets it, everyone gets it.

  • Big buttons

    Everything is big enough to hit, even without glasses.

  • Plain words

    No computer talk. It just says what happens: "Make a backup", "Choose a USB stick".

  • Nothing can break

    Every update is a complete new version. The previous one is always kept.

  • Nothing disappears

    BugOS never just throws something away. Not during a backup or an update either.

Switching

From Windows to BugOS in four steps.

The little BugOS Overstap program does the heavy lifting on your Windows computer. Nothing happens until you press a button.

  1. 1

    Check

    Can your computer run BugOS? Overstap checks it for you.

  2. 2

    Bring along

    Your documents, photos, music, bookmarks and wallpaper go to a USB drive.

  3. 3

    Make room

    Want to keep Windows? Then Overstap makes room, so BugOS fits next to it.

  4. 4

    Make a USB stick

    BugOS is downloaded, checked and put on a USB stick.

In BugOS you put everything back with one button, in the "Files from Windows" app.

Questions

Is BugOS really free?

Yes. BugOS is free and will stay free. The source code is open (GPL-3.0), so anyone can see how it works. There are no ads and you don't need an account.

Will my Windows programs work?

Many will. Double-click an .exe and BugOS starts it with Wine. For well-known programs, like Spotify or Zoom, BugOS offers the real BugOS version right away. Some programs, like Adobe or the newest Office, don't work; for those, BugOS suggests a good alternative.

Can I keep Windows?

Yes. BugOS Overstap can make Windows a bit smaller, so BugOS fits next to it. When you start up, you pick which one you want to use.

What if an update goes wrong?

Then you pick yesterday's version when you start up. Everything is back the way it was. You can also do it from Settings, with the "Back to previous version" button.

Is it good for gaming?

For sure. Steam with Proton is built in, controllers work right away and game mode gets the most out of your computer. Games with strict anti-cheat (some online shooters) sometimes don't work; check protondb.com to be sure.

Will my printer work?

Most printers work right away. Doesn't yours? Then open "Something is not working": BugOS checks it and helps you add the printer.

Is BugOS safe?

Yes. Programs can't change the system itself, apps run in their own closed-off space and updates come by themselves. On top of that, BugOS collects nothing about you.
